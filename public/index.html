WHO WE ARE

A school community where every learner matters.

Bright Angels Junior School is a learning community committed to helping every child grow in knowledge, confidence, character and creativity. We work with families to support each learner's development.

  • A welcoming and supportive learning environment
  • Strong foundations for lifelong learning
  • Partnership between school and family
  • Encouragement to try, improve and achieve

Our motto: If others can, we can.
